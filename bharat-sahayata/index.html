        About BharatSahayata

        Our Mission

        BharatSahayata is an independent, citizen-first discovery platform built to simplify access to welfare schemes, interest subventions, and credit guarantee programs offered by the Government of India and State Governments.

        Transparent 100-Point Matching Engine

        Our algorithmic engine evaluates an applicant’s occupation (25 pts), financial purpose (25 pts), annual family income (20 pts), age (10 pts), state/UT (10 pts), and social category (10 pts) against official eligibility rules. Schemes meeting a 40%+ match score are surfaced with transparent matching highlights.

        Privacy-First Architecture

        BharatSahayata operates locally in your browser. We never collect sensitive banking credentials, passwords, or personal identity numbers. All application links redirect users directly to official government portals (e.g. mudra.org.in, pmkisan.gov.in, vidyalakshmi.co.in).
